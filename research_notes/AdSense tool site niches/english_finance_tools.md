## Which finance calculator sub-niches are underserved?

### Takeaway
The clearest underserved pockets are SMB-finance and investor-lending calculators (MCA, invoice factoring, DSCR, equipment loans, BRRRR) and HECM/reverse-mortgage calculators. In these SERPs the results are almost all lenders' own sales pages or small specialist sites, and NerdWallet, Bankrate and Calculator.net are mostly absent. Brand-new, regulation-driven calculators (OBBBA deductions, the student-loan RAP plan, Trump Accounts) are also open: small exact-match-domain (EMD) sites rank within months. Consumer credit-card and retirement head terms (balance transfer, Roth conversion) are locked up by card issuers, brokerages and the big finance publishers.

### Inferences
- **Lender-held SERPs point to high-CPC but low-publisher-competition keywords.** This applies to MCA, factoring, DSCR, equipment and reverse mortgage. When lenders' own sales pages rank, the ad auction is full of the same lenders, so CPC should be high. The organic results, meanwhile, are thin sales tools that a neutral, better-built comparison calculator could outrank. The trade-off is that search volume for B2B and investor terms is probably small (unverified).
- **Reverse mortgage (HECM) looks like the strongest consumer mortgage sub-niche.**
  - The top 9 are specialist and exact-match sites rather than NerdWallet or Bankrate.
  - The audience is 62+ homeowners, a historically high-value lead-gen segment.
  - The HUD Principal Limit Factor tables and a limit that changes every year give a reason to keep updating the page.

- The same programmatic multi-calculator newcomer, richify.ai, appears in both the RAP and coast FIRE SERPs. That suggests a 2025-26 calculator site can rank across several finance sub-niches, though not necessarily for head terms.

## Which have strong CPC ($5–$50+) and weak SERP competition?

### Takeaway
The best combinations on the evidence gathered are:
- reverse mortgage / HECM calculators
- MCA-to-APR and invoice factoring calculators
- DSCR, BRRRR and rental investor calculators
- HELOC payment and HELOC-vs-cash-out variants
- equipment loan calculators

Their CPC is high because the advertisers are lenders. Organic competition is lender sales pages and small sites rather than NerdWallet or Bankrate. No per-keyword CPC could be verified; only industry-level benchmarks are available.

### Cited Findings
- **Industry average CPC:** a search summary of 2025-26 Google Ads benchmark pages (WordStream, LocaliQ, WebFX and others) gives **Finance & Insurance at about $3.44** and **Real Estate at about $5.38**. The same summary reports an "Insurance" figure of **$67.73**, but did not say which source it came from, so treat that one as unverified. — [WordStream 2025 benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks), [LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/), [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/ppc/much-cost-advertise-google-adwords/)
- **Top-of-range CPCs:** aggregator "most expensive keyword" lists report:
  - top CPCs of about **$44.28 for loan keywords, $47.12 for mortgage keywords and $54.91 for insurance keywords**
  - "mortgage refinance, home equity, and personal loan keywords" in a **$30–$80** range
  - lenders bidding up "home equity line of credit", "commercial mortgage" and "refinancing" because a funded loan earns thousands in fees
  - These are secondary sources, and which list gave which number is unclear. — [BloggersPassion](https://bloggerspassion.com/most-expensive-keywords/), [ppc.io](https://ppc.io/blog/high-cpc-keywords), [ClickPatrol](https://clickpatrol.com/most-expensive-google-keywords-in-2026-cpc-stats/)
- **Keyword difficulty:** Semrush reports that Finance has the **highest share of medium-difficulty keywords (63%)** of the industries studied, and close to a 1:1 ratio of easy to hard keywords. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-competition/)
- **Publisher RPM:** publisher-side estimates put finance RPM at **$15–$30+**, and "$20–$50 or higher" for highly specific finance pages with US traffic. One example cites 100k monthly US pageviews earning **$1,500–$4,000/month** from AdSense. These are low-quality, self-interested calculator-site sources; treat them as directional only. — [upgrowth.in](https://upgrowth.in/calculator/google-adsense-calculator/), [Ultimate Finance Calculator](https://www.ultimatefinancecalculator.com/calculators/google-adsense-earnings-calculator/)

## State- and country-specific variants

### Takeaway
Country-specific calculators are real opportunities, but the head terms for the biggest ones are already crowded with exact-match domains:
- UK stamp duty
- AU HECS
- the Canadian stress test

The openings are in their narrower variants and in schemes that changed in 2024-25:
- AU: the 5% Deposit Scheme / LMI savings, and the HECS marginal-repayment system
- CA: 30-year amortization for insured first-time buyers
- UK: stamp duty surcharge and edge cases (second home, limited company, mixed household)

### Cited Findings
- **UK stamp duty SERP ("stamp duty calculator UK 2026"):**
  - Crowded mix of MSE, Knight Frank, CBRE, a bridging-loan firm, Fox Davidson, and several exact-match domains: calculatemystampduty.co.uk, stampdutycalc.co.uk, sdltcalculator.com and stampdutycalculator.quest.
  - Pages are split by the three regimes: SDLT (England & NI), LBTT (Scotland) and LTT (Wales).
  - Current home-mover bands start at 0% up to £125,000, then 2% on the next £125,000 (rates effective 1 April 2025).
  - — [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/mortgages/stamp-duty/), [Knight Frank](https://www.knightfrank.co.uk/calculator/stamp-duty-calculator), [CBRE](https://www.cbreresidential.com/uk-resi/stamp-duty-calculator), [calculatemystampduty.co.uk](https://calculatemystampduty.co.uk/), [sdltcalculator.com](https://sdltcalculator.com/), [stampdutycalculator.quest](https://stampdutycalculator.quest/)

## Which tool types resist Google AI Overviews?

### Takeaway
The evidence is thin, but it points one way. AI Overviews cut clicks sharply on answer-in-a-paragraph queries, while multi-input, personalized calculators with changing rules or rate tables are less exposed. This is industry opinion, not a controlled measurement of calculator pages.

### Cited Findings
- Seer Interactive (September 2025 update; 3,119 search terms, 42 organizations, 25.1M organic impressions) found that organic CTR fell **61%** (from 1.76% to 0.61%) on queries showing an AI Overview. Paid CTR fell **68%** (from 19.7% to 6.34%). Brands cited inside the AI Overview earned **35% more organic clicks** and **91% more paid clicks**. — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update) (via a search summary; the direct fetch was blocked)
- Industry commentary says queries fully answerable in a paragraph lose the most traffic. By contrast, "queries that require deeper engagement, personal judgment, or interactive tools remain relatively protected", and tools and calculators "cannot be replicated easily by AI". — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-the-end-of-traditional-ctr-and-how-to-adapt-in-2025), [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-overviews-13-percent-queries-seo-traffic-impact)

### Inferences
- **More resilient** (many inputs, personalized, rule tables that change):
  - HECM proceeds using HUD PLF tables, age and limit
  - DSCR/BRRRR deal analysis
  - OBBBA deduction stacking (tips + overtime + car interest + senior, with MAGI phaseouts)
  - RAP vs IBR payment comparison
  - S-corp salary/distribution split
  - quarterly estimated tax with safe harbor
  - UK SDLT surcharge combinations, the CA stress test with 30-year amortization, AU HECS marginal repayment
  - Amortization schedules, charts and scenario comparisons are what AI Overviews do poorly.
- **Less resilient** (single formula or one-line answer):
  - "what is DTI", "how to calculate cap rate"
  - simple inflation or APY conversions

## 2025-2026 regulatory and market changes creating fresh demand

### Takeaway
Several 2025-26 policy changes created brand-new calculator keywords where small sites and exact-match domains are ranking now:
- **US OBBBA:** no tax on tips, no tax on overtime, the car-loan-interest deduction, the senior deduction, and Trump Accounts
- **US student loans:** the RAP plan (launched 1 July 2026)
- **US reverse mortgages:** the 2026 HECM limit
- **Australia:** the HECS marginal system and the expanded 5% Deposit Scheme
- **Canada:** 30-year insured amortization

The window is short. Exact-match competitors appear within weeks to months, and most OBBBA provisions expire after 2028.

### Cited Findings
- **OBBBA (now branded "Working Families Tax Cuts") deductions, claimed on the new Schedule 1-A:**
  - **Tips:** capped at $25,000, phasing out above $150k MAGI ($300k joint).
  - **Overtime:** capped at $12,500 single / $25,000 joint, for FLSA overtime premium only.
  - **Car loan interest:** up to $10,000 a year for 2025–2028, on new US-assembled vehicles. It phases out above $100k/$200k MAGI, dropping $200 per $1,000, and is gone at $150k/$250k. RVs and campers are excluded.
  - Schedule 1-A also carries the **enhanced senior deduction**.

### Inferences
- **The new-regulation pattern is consistent.** Within weeks to months of a rule change, SERPs fill with tax-prep brands plus 2–5 exact-match domains (notaxonovertime…, autoloandeductible…, rapplancalc/rapstudentloan, trumpaccounttools). A new site can win these only by moving fast or by being clearly better, for example combined tools such as an "OBBBA Schedule 1-A all-deductions calculator" or "RAP vs IBR vs PSLF comparison".
- **Seasonality and expiry.** OBBBA deductions are time-limited (2025–2028), and their searches will peak in tax season (January–April). The car-loan deduction also ties into auto-purchase intent, where auto lenders and dealers advertise, so CPC is probably moderate to high.
- **RAP is probably the most durable new student-loan keyword set.** It is permanent for new borrowers, but the SERP is already crowded with specialists. Differentiate on RAP vs IBR/PSLF comparisons and on Parent PLUS or grad borrower scenarios.
- **Evergreen demand.** Annual limit and threshold changes (HECM limit, HECS threshold, 2026 tax brackets) create yearly freshness, which suits a calculator site that updates reliably.
