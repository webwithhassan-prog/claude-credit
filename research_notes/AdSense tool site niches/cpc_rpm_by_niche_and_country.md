# AdSense payouts (CPC, page RPM) by vertical and country, 2025-2026, for a free online tools/calculators site

**Research date:** 2026-09-25.

**Access caveat:** WebFetch was blocked by the egress proxy for every domain tried: wordstream.com, localiq.com, searchenginejournal.com, ppc.io, techconda.com, support.google.com, ybierling.com, get-ryze.ai and theedigital.com. All figures below therefore come from **WebSearch result summaries** of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Treat exact decimals as "reported by the summary". Spot-check the primary pages before quoting them externally.

**Reliability tags used below:**
- **[DATASET]**: industry benchmark dataset with a stated methodology.
- **[KW-TOOL]**: keyword-tool-derived CPC (Semrush, Ahrefs, Keyword Planner), usually via an aggregator.
- **[VENDOR-EST]**: a vendor or blog "benchmark" with opaque methodology.
- **[3P-EST]**: third-party traffic or revenue estimate for a named site.
- **[ANECDOTAL]**: self-reported by a publisher or forum post.
- **[GOOGLE]**: Google primary source.

**Key distinction:** *advertiser CPC* (what advertisers pay per click, mostly on Search) vs *publisher earnings* (AdSense page RPM). AdSense display CPCs are a small fraction of Search CPCs, so Search CPC benchmarks are useful only as a **relative** signal of vertical value.

## Q1. Which advertiser verticals have the highest Google Ads CPC in 2025-2026, and what are typical ranges?

### Takeaway
On industry-average Search CPC, **Legal ($9.21 in 2025, $9.87 in 2026), Home & Home Improvement ($7.85, then $8.33) and Dentists ($7.85, then $8.00)** lead the WordStream/LocaliQ benchmarks. Education was $6.23 in 2025. The all-industry average was $5.26 in 2025 and $5.42 in 2026. At the **keyword** level, insurance, mortgage and legal terms dominate: about $47-55 top CPCs per category, and individual legal terms reach hundreds of dollars. Pure SaaS/B2B tech averages about $9-15. Finance & Insurance looks deceptively low as an *industry average* ($3.44 in 2026) because the benchmark is built from SMB/local accounts.

### Cited Findings
- **WordStream/LocaliQ 2025 (published 2025) [DATASET].**
  - Average Search CPC across industries was **$5.26**, up 12.88% YoY, and CPC rose in **87%** of industries.
  - Industry averages ranged from **$1.16 (e-commerce) to $9.21 (Attorneys & Legal Services)**.
  - Lowest: Arts & Entertainment $1.60, Restaurants & Food $2.05, Travel $2.12.
  - Education & Instruction **$6.23**; Dentists & Dental Services and Home & Home Improvement **$7.85 each**.
  - Real Estate had the largest YoY CPC increase (+27.27%) and a CPL of $100.48. Business Services had among the highest CPLs ($103.54). Health & Fitness CPC rose about 23.41%. Finance & Insurance conversion rate was 2.55%.
  - Sources: [WordStream 2025 benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks); [Success Quarterly summary](https://successquarterly.com/wordstreams-2025-search-ad-benchmarks/)
- **WordStream/LocaliQ 2026 [DATASET].**
  - Sample: **13,474 US-based Search campaigns** (Google plus Microsoft Ads), 23 industries, **April 2025 to March 2026**.
  - Average CPC **$5.42**; average conversion rate 8.18%. Average CPL fell for the first time in five years.
  - Highest CPCs: **Attorneys & Legal $9.87** (also the highest CPL at $131.63), **Home & Home Improvement $8.33**, **Dentists $8.00**.
  - **Finance & Insurance $3.44** (CVR 2.64%); **Real Estate $3.22**.
  - Lowest: Arts & Entertainment $1.63, Restaurants $2.05, Travel $2.14.
  - Education & Instruction had the biggest CPC decrease (**-22.79% YoY**). Automotive (For Sale) CPC fell about 5%.
  - Sources: [WordStream 2026 benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2026-google-ads-benchmarks); [LocaliQ 2026 search benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/); [LocaliQ real estate benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/real-estate-search-advertising-benchmarks/)
- **Keyword-level "most expensive" data [KW-TOOL via aggregator; July 2026 page; underlying tool not verified].**
  - Top CPC by category: **insurance $54.91, mortgage $47.12, attorney $47.07**.
  - Terms like "auto insurance quote", "buy life insurance" and "health insurance plan" are cited at **$60-90/click**. Mortgage and commercial-loan terms often exceed $50-100.
  - Legal outliers: "best mesothelioma lawyer" up to **$935/click**, "truck accident lawyer" **$500+**.
  - Sources: [ppc.io, Most Expensive Keywords (July 2026)](https://ppc.io/blog/high-cpc-keywords); [ClickPatrol, 2025](https://clickpatrol.com/most-expensive-google-keywords-in-2025-cpc-stats/); [WordStream most expensive keywords](https://www.wordstream.com/articles/most-expensive-keywords)
- **B2B SaaS [VENDOR-EST; agency benchmark, sample not verified].**
  - Average B2B SaaS CPC **$5.34** (+29% YoY). B2B tech averages **$8.86** and "pure SaaS" **$15.36**.
  - By sub-vertical: HR Tech $14.32, Cybersecurity $10.44, Infrastructure $3.15.
  - Sources: [Powered by Search, B2B SaaS Google Ads benchmarks 2026](https://www.poweredbysearch.com/learn/b2b-saas-google-ads-stats-benchmarks/); [GrowthSpree SaaS benchmarks 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/saas-google-ads-benchmarks-2026-cpc-cpl-ctr-conversion-rate-by-vertical)

### Inferences
- **Relative vertical value (advertiser side), highest to lowest:**
  1. Legal (injury, mesothelioma, DUI, settlement)
  2. Insurance (auto, life, health/Medicare)
  3. Mortgage/refinance/loans
  4. Pure SaaS/B2B software
  5. Home improvement/solar/roofing and dental
  6. Education (degree programs)
  7. Real estate
  8. Health & fitness
  9. Automotive
  10. Travel, food, arts & entertainment

  This combines the industry averages and the keyword-level data.
- **Finance & Insurance averages only $3.44 in WordStream 2026, yet insurance and mortgage keywords top $47-55.** The WordStream/LocaliQ sample is mainly SMB/local advertisers, such as local insurance agents, rather than national carriers and lenders. So the industry average understates what big finance advertisers bid on high-intent terms. This is my interpretation and not stated by the source. For a calculator site, the keyword-level data is more relevant, because pages like "mortgage calculator" or "auto insurance cost calculator" sit next to the most expensive queries.
- **Education's 2026 CPC.** Applying the -22.79% change to the 2025 figure of $6.23 gives about **$4.81**. This is my arithmetic, not a published number.

### Gaps
- I could not open the full 2025 or 2026 WordStream/LocaliQ tables because they were egress-blocked. Per-industry CPCs for Finance & Insurance (2025), Business Services, Automotive, Health & Fitness, Career & Employment, Shopping and Personal Services are missing.
- No verified 2025-2026 CPC data for **Medicare/health insurance** or **solar** keywords specifically. A search found agency pages but no numbers.
- No direct Semrush or Ahrefs 2025-2026 "most expensive keywords" study was retrieved. The keyword-level figures come via aggregators (ppc.io, ClickPatrol).

## Q2. What share of advertiser spend do AdSense publishers receive, and what page RPMs do tool/calculator sites actually get (finance vs utility)?

### Takeaway
Publishers net **about 68%** of what advertisers spend when the buyer is Google Ads: 80% of the net after Google Ads' about-15% buy-side fee. Realistic **AdSense** page RPMs by site type:
- **Finance-heavy calculator sites with Tier-1 traffic:** mid-teens to about $40+. Vendor data claims $30-60 for finance in Tier 1.
- **Premium networks (Raptive, Mediavine) in personal finance:** $15-40+ per 1,000 sessions, with top performers above $55.
- **Generic utility tools with global traffic:** typically about **$0.5-3** per 1,000 pageviews.

The best public proxy for a large multi-vertical calculator site is **Omni Calculator**, estimated at about **$21-26 per 1,000 visits**. That is a third-party estimate.

### Cited Findings
- **Revenue share (effective 2024) [GOOGLE].**
  - For AdSense for Content, publishers receive **80%** of revenue after the buy-side platform's fee.
  - When advertisers buy through **Google Ads**, Google Ads retains about **15%**, so the publisher gets 0.80 x 0.85 = **about 68%**. Google framed this as unchanged overall from the previous 68%.
  - AdSense also moved from per-click to **per-impression (CPM) payment** in 2024.
  - Sources: [AdSense Help: revenue share](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/180195?hl=en); [Google Keyword blog: evolving how publishers monetize](https://blog.google/products/adsense/evolving-how-publishers-monetize-with-adsense/); [9to5Google, Nov 2023](https://9to5google.com/2023/11/02/google-adsense-payments-2024/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/adsense-publisher-payments/500160/)
- **AdSense content CPC is far below Search CPC [VENDOR-EST; data likely 2022, not verified as 2026].** Partnerkin reports AdSense RPM of **$4.59-6.15** for the top English-speaking countries and about **$2 average** for other English-speaking countries. US **publisher-side CPC was $0.61**, then Australia $0.57. — [Partnerkin, AdSense RPM rates by country](https://partnerkin.com/en/blog/articles/adsense_rpm_rates_by_country)
- **Session metrics retired [GOOGLE].** Google is retiring session-related metrics (such as ad session RPM) in AdSense from **September 2025**. Page RPM remains the main publisher metric. — [AdSense Help: Ad session RPM](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/6373314?hl=en)
- **Premium-network finance RPM [ANECDOTAL/VENDOR-EST; 2025-2026].**
  - Personal finance display RPM is cited at **$15-40 per 1,000 sessions**, with best performers above **$55**.
  - Gaming is cited at **$2-6**.
  - Mediavine is cited at $15-40+ (50k sessions/month minimum) and Raptive at $18-50+ (about 100k pageviews minimum). Raptive reportedly beats Mediavine by 15-30% in finance and tech.
  - One Raptive publisher reported **$47-57 RPM (avg $54) in Oct-Dec 2025**. Q4 is seasonally inflated.
  - Sources: [ToolSignal, Display ad RPM by niche 2026](https://toolsignal.site/articles/blog-display-ad-rpm-by-niche-2026); [EarnifyHub](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/blogging/mediavine-vs-raptive-vs-ezoic-rpm); [This Week in Blogging, Mediavine vs Raptive](https://thisweekinblogging.com/mediavine-vs-raptive/)
- **Adstimate "Highest paying AdSense niches 2026" [VENDOR-EST].**
  - The site claims an "internal benchmark dataset" aggregated from thousands of AdSense accounts. Its methodology cannot be verified, and the site sells RPM estimation.
  - **Finance:** multiplier **3.0x** the average site, RPM **$30-60 for Tier-1 traffic**.
  - **Insurance:** 2.4x. **Legal:** 2.3x.
  - **New sites** earn **30-40% less** until authority builds.
  - Sources: [Adstimate, highest paying AdSense niches 2026](https://adstimate.com/blog/highest-paying-adsense-niches.html); [Adstimate, education RPM $15-30](https://adstimate.com/blog/niche/education-adsense-rpm.html)
- **Generic RPM ranges [VENDOR-EST, unsourced].** Entertainment/viral with global traffic about **$1-4**; recipes/lifestyle **$4-10**; finance/insurance/legal/B2B software **$15-40+** with US traffic. — [Ultimate Finance Calculator, AdSense calculator page](https://www.ultimatefinancecalculator.com/calculators/google-adsense-earnings-calculator/)
- **Ad-network comparison study, MotionInvest (Feb-Aug 2023, older data) [DATASET, small sample].**
  - Sample: 13 niches, 3-5 marketplace sites per niche, 6 months.
  - **AdSense average EPMV $6.62 vs Ezoic $12.92.** Ezoic earned 50-300% more.
  - Careers/Employment had the highest average EPMV across platforms (**$25.5**). Health was lowest (**$8.5**). Outdoor on Mediavine averaged **$29.50** EPMV, 1,375% above AdSense.
  - EPMV is per 1,000 *visitors*, not pageviews.
  - Source: [MotionInvest, Finding the best ad network](https://www.motioninvest.com/finding-the-best-ad-network-changing-platforms-can-increase-website-revenue-by-300/)
- **Ezoic ranges [VENDOR-EST].** EPMV about **$5-25** in valuable niches with premium traffic, falling to **$1-5** with low-value-country traffic. An Ezoic community thread is titled "Can EPMV on my niche go above $1-2?". — [Hostragons](https://www.hostragons.com/en/blog/ezoic-vs-mediavine-application-criteria-earnings-comparison/); [Ezoic Community thread](https://community.ezoic.com/d/12783-can-epmv-on-my-niche-go-above-1-2)
- **Viewability benchmarks (the relevant mechanism for short tool sessions) [DATASET/VENDOR].**
  - IAS reported **67.9%** global display viewability and 79.7% video (July 2026). DoubleVerify reports **66%** display.
  - Google Active View standard: **50% of pixels for 1 continuous second** (display). Large-format display counts at 30% of pixels.
  - Raising viewability from about 40% to 70% "unlocks" demand. Optimizing viewability reportedly yields about **+30% RPM** (vendor claim).
  - Sticky units reach **more than 80%** viewability.
  - Sources: [Publift, ad viewability optimization 2026](https://www.publift.com/blog/ad-viewability-optimization); [PubPower](https://pubpower.io/blog/best-ad-placement-for-viewability/)
- **Low-RPM anecdote from an Indie Hackers tool/content creator [ANECDOTAL, 2024].** AdSense revenue fell from **$800 to $200/month overnight** at the same traffic. The creator then switched to direct sponsorships at **$30+ CPM versus about $0.50 on AdSense**, reaching $3k/month. — [Indie Hackers post](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/from-200-to-3k-month-why-i-ditched-adsense-for-direct-ad-sales-with-numbers-d1098e3aae)

### Inferences
- **Publisher-side arithmetic.** Search CPC benchmarks overstate display economics by roughly an order of magnitude. A US AdSense content click was about **$0.61** in the Partnerkin data, versus a $5.42 Search average. That means "68% of a $50 insurance CPC" is **not** what a calculator page earns per click. Use advertiser CPC only to rank verticals.
- **Tool pages and viewability.** Tool pages typically have one pageview per session, fast task completion and little scrolling. That limits below-the-fold impressions and viewable time, so tool sites likely monetize closer to the lower viewability band (about 40-60%) unless the layout keeps ads in view: an above-the-tool unit, sticky or anchor units, and results-adjacent units. This is an inference. No source quantifies tool-page viewability specifically.
- **Omni Calculator implied revenue per 1,000 visits** (see Q5). About $21-26 per 1,000 visits for a broad calculator catalogue with heavy finance, health and education content and substantial US/English traffic. Because it is per *visit* and visits include some multi-page sessions, per-pageview RPM would be lower. Consider it an upper-mid benchmark for a mature, authority calculator brand, not for a new site.

### Gaps
- **No verified first-party income reports** (Reddit, IndieHackers or X) giving AdSense page RPM for a *specific* utility tool site: unit converter, word counter, image compressor or QR generator. Reddit fetches were not possible, and search summaries did not surface concrete numbers.
- **No Raptive or Mediavine published RPM-by-niche tables.** The numbers above are from third-party blogs.
- **No source quantifies** session length, pages per session or viewability for tool/calculator pages versus articles.
- **AdSense for Search revenue share** was not confirmed in this session because support.google.com was blocked.

## Q3. RPM by country and language: which have the highest RPM, and how much does traffic mix matter?

### Takeaway
For web display, the **US is consistently the top or near-top market**, followed by **Australia, Canada, UK**, the **Nordics, Switzerland, Luxembourg** and **Germany/Austria/Netherlands/Ireland/NZ/Singapore**. Several YouTube-based datasets put **Norway, Denmark, Switzerland and Luxembourg above the US** per 1,000 views, but that is YouTube data, not display. Non-English-language sites historically earn much less per view than English sites, because each small-language market has less advertiser inventory. India, Pakistan, Indonesia and the Philippines earn roughly **5-50x less** than the US per 1,000 views. Traffic mix is therefore often a bigger lever than niche for a tool site.

### Cited Findings
- **Tier model [VENDOR-EST, 2026].**
  - **Tier 1** (US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany): RPM **$15-45**.
  - **Tier 2** (Brazil, Mexico, Spain, Italy, much of Eastern Europe): **$5-15**.
  - **Tier 3** (India, Pakistan, Philippines, much of Africa): **$0.50-5**.
  - These ranges look finance-skewed or optimistic for general tools.
  - Source: [Adstimate, AdSense RPM by country 2026](https://adstimate.com/blog/adsense-rpm-by-country.html)
- **AdSense CPC ranking by country [DATASET-ish aggregator; data "updated January 2024", some countries 2023].**
  - Order listed: Iceland, Albania, Luxembourg, **United States, Australia**, Montenegro, **UK, Canada, Austria, Finland**, Slovenia, Slovakia, **New Zealand, Sweden, Ireland**, Croatia, **Denmark, Singapore**, South Africa, **Netherlands, Norway**, Bahamas, **Germany, Belgium, Switzerland**, Spain, Djibouti.
  - The presence of Albania, Montenegro and Djibouti suggests small-sample noise.
  - Source: [World Population Review, AdSense CPC rates by country 2026](https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/adsense-cpc-rates-by-country)
- **Partnerkin [VENDOR-EST; likely 2022 data].** Top English-speaking countries' AdSense RPM **$4.59-6.15**; other English-speaking countries about **$2**. Publisher CPC: US **$0.61**, Australia **$0.57**. — [Partnerkin](https://partnerkin.com/en/blog/articles/adsense_rpm_rates_by_country)
- **YouTube AdSense RPM, Dynamoi (84 markets, updated through 2026-09-10) [DATASET, YouTube music "Art Track" distribution data, not web display].**
  - **Denmark $8.05, Luxembourg $7.71, Australia $7.52, Norway $7.14, Switzerland $6.07, US $5.58.** Japan $3.14; Mexico $0.35; Morocco $0.28; Serbia $0.48.
  - The median market is about $1.00, and the top market is about 8x the median.
  - A separate Dynamoi statistics page reports different figures: **Norway $16.33, Switzerland $12.64, Germany $10.36, UK $9.35, US $6.66**, Indonesia $3.49, Brazil $3.24, India $1.07. Pakistan is about $0.50-2.
  - The two Dynamoi pages conflict, probably because they use different content types or periods. Use both only for relative ordering.
  - Sources: [Dynamoi YouTube AdSense RPM, 84 markets](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm); [Dynamoi YouTube RPM by country](https://dynamoi.com/learn/statistics/youtube-rpm-by-country); [Dynamoi Japan](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/jp); [Dynamoi Mexico](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/mx)
- **YouTube CPM (advertiser side) [VENDOR-EST, 2026].** US about **$14.67**, Australia **$13.30**, Switzerland **$12.98** per 1,000 ad impressions. — [MilX, countries with highest YouTube CPM 2026](https://milx.app/en/cases/in-what-countries-cpm-are-the-highest)
- **"European elite" traffic [VENDOR-EST].** Germany, Switzerland and Norway traffic is claimed to yield **$15-60+ RPM**, and **$100+** in finance or law. This looks optimistic and unverified. — [Dicloak, AdSense RPM by country 2025](https://dicloak.com/blog-detail/google-adsense-rpm-by-country-2025-how-to-maximize-revenue-across-countries)
- **RPM by language [ANECDOTAL, single-site data, older (likely pre-2020), title "30 fold increase"].**
  - **English above $1** per 1,000 views; **German below $0.5**; **French below $0.5**; **Japanese about $0.2**.
  - The same summary notes a Japan **finance** blog earning **$8 RPM**.
  - Source: [Y. Bierling, AdSense RPM rates by language](https://www.ybierling.com/en/blog-marketing-adsense-cpm-rates-by-country)
- **German-language site [ANECDOTAL, forum, date unclear].** A German site earns **EUR 3-7 RPM** on AdSense. The explanation given is smaller language inventory, strict privacy/consent rules and conservative bidding. Moving to a premium partner at 50k+ sessions reportedly raises RPM **150-300%**. — [Builder Society thread](https://www.buildersociety.com/threads/best-display-ad-companies-for-german-website-sharing-my-rpms-with-raptive.7312/); [Global Fin Info, Germany ad platforms](https://www.global-fin-info.com/advertising-platforms-for-websites-in-germany/)
- **India vs US [VENDOR-EST/ANECDOTAL].**
  - Median US finance RPM about **$25** vs India finance about **$5**, a **5x** gap. High-end US finance at **$45** vs broad Indian traffic at **$3** is **15x**.
  - India finance RPM is cited at **INR 250-830 ($3-10)**.
  - Indian traffic generally earns **$1-4 RPM**.
  - A Medium post argues a US visitor is worth **up to 50x** an Indian visitor.
  - Sources: [ArbHunter, best countries 2026](https://arbhunter.dev/blog/best-countries-ad-arbitrage-2026); [Rupee Logics](https://www.rupeelogics.in/blogs/adsense-blog); [Medium, R. Dutta](https://medium.com/@dutta.rajib/the-unspoken-truth-about-adsense-earnings-why-a-us-visitor-is-worth-50x-one-from-india-25d7d67bf9c7)
- **Region view [VENDOR-EST].** Americas traffic (especially US/CA) earns **2-3x** Asia-Pacific or EMEA. A **$5 page RPM** is "strong" for India-heavy traffic but only "average" for US/UK. — [Convertixy AdSense RPM calculator](https://convertixy.com/adsense-rpm-calculator)

### Inferences
- **Country ranking for a tools site, expected AdSense page RPM, highest to lowest:**
  1. **US**
  2. **Australia, Canada, UK**
  3. **Switzerland, Norway, Denmark, Luxembourg, Ireland, NZ, Sweden, Netherlands, Austria, Finland, Singapore**
  4. **Germany, Belgium, France, Japan, UAE** (UAE is not covered by any retrieved source)
  5. Southern and Eastern Europe, Brazil, Mexico
  6. **Indonesia, Philippines, India, Pakistan**, most of Africa

  This ordering synthesizes the WPR CPC ranking, the tier model and the YouTube proxies. The ranking within tiers is weakly supported.
- **High-income non-English markets.** They can match or exceed the US per 1,000 views on YouTube. For **web display in the local language**, anecdotal data shows much lower RPM than English/US: German EUR 3-7, Japanese about $0.2 historically. This is because each language pool has fewer AdSense advertisers. The upside of localizing a tool into German, Nordic languages or Japanese is real but smaller than the YouTube numbers suggest. The strongest non-US play is likely **English pages consumed by UK, CA, AU, IE, NZ and Nordic users**, who often use English tools.
- **Traffic-mix math (illustrative, my arithmetic).** Assume a US RPM of $10 and India at $0.50, a 20x gap within the cited 5-50x range.
  - 30% US + 70% India gives 0.3 x 10 + 0.7 x 0.5 = **$3.35** blended.
  - 70% US + 30% India gives **$7.15**.
  - Mix therefore moves blended RPM by 2x or more, as much as niche choice does. Generic utilities (PDF, image, QR) attract heavily global, South and Southeast Asian traffic, which is my inference from typical search-demand geography and not sourced here.

### Gaps
- No **2025-2026 first-party web (non-YouTube) AdSense page-RPM table by country**. Most "RPM by country" posts are SEO content with no methodology. techconda.com's 2025 benchmark page was blocked.
- **UAE, Singapore, Japan, France, Belgium** web-display RPMs: no specific numbers found.
- The language-RPM data (Bierling) is old and single-site. No 2025-2026 replication was found.

## Q4. Which verticals are restricted or limited in AdSense or Google Ads, and what does that mean for niche selection?

### Takeaway
Crypto, gambling, payday/short-term loans, prescription drugs, unapproved supplements, weapons, tobacco, alcohol and adult content are restricted or prohibited on the **advertiser** side. The publisher side has matching restrictions (e.g., online gambling). These rules shrink the advertiser pool and can lower fill and RPM for tool pages in those topics. Mainstream finance (mortgage, insurance, savings, tax) is **not** restricted to the same degree and remains the best high-CPC space for calculators.

### Cited Findings
- **Online gambling publisher restriction [GOOGLE, via search summary].**
  - As of **March 2025**, Google updated the **Publisher Restriction for Online Gambling**. It applies when gambling content is shown to users **outside allowed countries**.
  - Allowed countries: Australia, Brazil, Canada, Colombia, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Netherlands, Philippines, South Korea, Spain, Turkey, UK, US.
  - Restricted content categories include sexual content, shocking content, explosives, guns and gun parts, other weapons, tobacco, recreational drugs, alcohol, online gambling, prescription drugs, and unapproved pharmaceuticals and supplements.
  - Sources: [AdSense policy change log](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9336650?hl=en); [Google Ads policies](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6008942?hl=en)
- **Gambling definition expanded [VENDOR summary of policy, 2025-2026].** The definition now includes games with **virtual currencies or items with real-world value**, covering crypto casinos, skin gambling and NFT games. — [Blockchain-Ads, Google gambling ads 2026](https://www.blockchain-ads.com/post/gambling-ads-google); [Fortis Media, 2025](https://www.fortismedia.com/en/articles/google-ads-gambling-policy/)
- **Crypto [VENDOR summary of policy].** Crypto advertisers must be **Google-certified**, **licensed by financial authorities** and target **approved countries only**. These are described as among the strictest Google Ads rules. — [MegaDigital, Google Ads crypto policy 2026](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/google-ads-crypto-content-policy/)
- **Payday and binary options [VENDOR summary].** Short-term, high-APR payday loans are prohibited in many regions. Binary options and payday loans are described as fully prohibited. — [Agrowth, Google Ads restricted content 2025](https://agrowth.io/blogs/google-ads/google-ads-restricted-content); [Blockchain-Ads, finance ads policy](https://www.blockchain-ads.com/post/finance-ads-google-policy)

### Inferences
- **Niches to avoid or deprioritize for AdSense-monetized tools:**
  - Crypto profit/mining/staking calculators: small certified-advertiser pool and country-limited.
  - Gambling tools (odds converters, betting, parlay calculators): publisher restriction outside allowed countries, and no Google Ads demand in many markets.
  - Payday/short-term loan calculators.
  - Prescription-drug dosage tools: restricted pharma ads, plus YMYL and approval risk.
  - Supplement dosing, and alcohol/BAC calculators: alcohol is a restricted category.

  These pages risk limited ads, lower fill, or AdSense approval problems. The better finance choices are **mortgage, refinance, auto loan, insurance, retirement, tax, salary/paycheck and business tools**, where major licensed advertisers bid freely.
- **Crypto CPCs.** Crypto keywords may show high Search CPCs, but restricted demand means display fill for crypto tool pages is unlikely to reflect them. This is an inference; no RPM data was found.

### Gaps
- No quantified data on **fill-rate or RPM reduction** for restricted-category pages.

## Q5. How much do utility tools earn compared with finance calculators, and is high-volume, low-CPC traffic viable?

### Takeaway
Revenue estimates for real tool sites exist only as **third-party estimates**, which are low reliability. They suggest:
- A **pure utility tool** (image resizer) earns about **$3 per 1,000 visits**.
- A **broad calculator brand with heavy finance and health content** (Omni Calculator) earns about **$21-26 per 1,000 visits**.

That is roughly a **7-10x gap per visit**. High-volume utilities can still produce six to seven figures a year, but only at millions of monthly visits. Their revenue depends on global traffic mix and ad layout far more than a finance calculator's does.

### Cited Findings
- **Omni Calculator [3P-EST plus GOOGLE case study].**
  - Google's publisher story: grew from **20,000 to more than 18 million monthly visits**, is monetized "predominantly with ads" via **AdSense**, and used ad revenue to hire about **70 employees**.
  - BoringCashCow: **23.1M monthly visits (early 2024)** and estimated **$500k/month**.
  - Growjo/Dealroom-type estimate: **$4.2M/year** revenue. About **200M visits in 2023** and **3,700+ calculators**.
  - Sources: [Google for Publishers, Omni Calculator](https://www.google.com/ads/publisher/stories/omni_calculator/); [BoringCashCow, calculators site 23.1M visits](https://boringcashcow.com/view/calculators-website-generates-231-million-visits-a-month); [Growjo, Omni Calculator](https://growjo.com/company/Omni_Calculator); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/omni_calculator)
- **Free image resizer [3P-EST].** ImageResizer is estimated at **3M visits/month** and "potential" of about **$100k/year**. The figure was estimated with Google's AdSense calculator, not reported by the owner. — [BoringCashCow, image resizer $100k/yr](https://boringcashcow.com/view/boring-business-free-online-image-resizer-makes-100k-a-year)
- **WordCounter.net [3P-EST, low reliability].** Over **15M visits/month** and an estimated **$6.4M/year**. The summary says it monetizes via an **affiliate program** (e.g., writing-tool affiliates), not only display ads. — [BoringCashCow, word counting site $6.4M/yr](https://boringcashcow.com/view/free-word-counting-website-generates-64-million-a-year)
- **QR code generator ME-QR [GOOGLE case study].** Recouped its initial investment **within the first month** of AdSense. It bootstrapped traffic with about **$1,500 of Google Ads**. The general AdSense range cited alongside it is **$0.2-2.5 per 1,000 views**, depending on content, geography, time on site and device. — [Google for Publishers, ME-QR](https://www.google.com/ads/publisher/stories/me_qr/); [BoringCashCow, QR generator](https://boringcashcow.com/view/free-qr-code-generator-generates-millions-a-year); [Publisher Collective](https://www.publisher-collective.com/blog/adsense-revenue-calculator)
- **Finance calculator traffic scale [ANECDOTAL/tool-estimated].** One **SIP (systematic investment plan) calculator** page drew about **3.5M organic visits**, with an estimated traffic value of **$128k**. One site's calculator subfolder had **216 pages and 8.6M traffic**. These are Ahrefs-style traffic-value estimates, not revenue. — [search summary referencing Ahrefs blog content](https://ahrefs.com/blog/?p=183712)
- **Seasonality [VENDOR].** RPM peaks in **Q4 (Oct-Dec)** and drops sharply in **January/Q1** as budgets reset. No reliable percentage was found. — [Mediavine, ad revenue seasons](https://www.mediavine.com/blog/ad-revenue-seasons/); [Freestar, January](https://freestar.com/why-january-is-a-chill-month-for-publisher-ad-revenue-seasonal-trends-explained/); [Refinery89, Q1 RPM drop](https://refinery89.com/q1-rpm-drop-what-publishers-can-do-about-it/)

### Inferences
- **Implied revenue per 1,000 visits (my arithmetic on third-party estimates):**

| Site / type | Estimate basis | Implied $ per 1,000 visits | Reliability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Omni Calculator (multi-vertical calculators, finance/health-heavy) | $500k/mo ÷ 23.1M visits | **about $21.6** | 3P-EST |
| Omni Calculator (alternative) | $4.2M/yr ÷ about 200M visits (2023) | **about $21** | 3P-EST |
| ImageResizer (pure utility) | $100k/yr ÷ 36M visits/yr | **about $2.8** | 3P-EST (Google calculator) |
| WordCounter.net (utility plus affiliate) | $6.4M/yr ÷ 180M visits/yr | about $35.6 | **Implausible for display alone**. Likely inflated or affiliate-driven; do not use as an RPM benchmark |
| Generic AdSense range | vendor | $0.2-2.5 per 1,000 views | VENDOR-EST |

- **Tool niches ranked by expected AdSense page RPM (US/Tier-1-heavy traffic). Indicative ranges, my synthesis:**

| Tier | Tool examples | Expected RPM |
|---|---|---|
| A (highest) | Mortgage/refinance, home affordability, loan/auto-loan, insurance cost/needs (life, auto, health/Medicare), retirement/401k/annuity, debt payoff/credit card, tax/paycheck, business/SaaS-adjacent tools (payroll, invoice, SaaS pricing/ROI, business loan), and legal-adjacent calculators (personal injury settlement, child support) | about **$10-40+**. Premium networks: $15-55 finance |
| B | Real estate (rent vs buy, closing costs), home improvement/construction (concrete, roofing, paint, flooring, solar savings, HVAC sizing), education/careers (salary, GPA/grade, student loans; salary/career pages lean toward Tier A), automotive (fuel cost, car depreciation, lease) | about **$5-15** |
| C | Health/fitness (BMI, calorie, TDEE, pregnancy/due date): high volume, moderate CPC, MotionInvest found health lowest-EPMV. Date/time and math/science calculators | about **$2-8** |
| D (lowest) | Pure utilities with global, often Tier-3-heavy traffic: unit converters, word/character counters, image compressors/resizers/converters, PDF tools, QR generators, text/case converters, developer utilities (JSON, base64) | about **$0.5-3** blended |

Anchors for these tiers: the Search CPC hierarchy from Q1; the Adstimate multipliers (finance 3.0x, insurance 2.4x, legal 2.3x); premium-network finance data ($15-55); the MotionInvest EPMVs (careers $25.5, health $8.5, AdSense average $6.62); and the Omni and image-resizer implied figures.

- **Is high-volume, low-CPC traffic viable?** Yes, but only at scale.
  - At about $2-3 per 1,000 pageviews, $1,000/month takes about **350k-500k pageviews/month**.
  - At about $20 (Tier-A finance calculator, US traffic), the same $1,000 takes about **50k**.
  - Utility sites therefore need **7-10x the traffic** for the same revenue. Their demand is more global, so blended RPM erodes further.
  - Their advantages: high search volume, evergreen and repeat usage, easier links and virality, and less YMYL scrutiny.
  - A common hybrid: a utility "traffic engine" plus finance or business calculators and affiliate/SaaS offers. WordCounter.net's affiliate model points to this.
  - Utility tools' short, task-focused sessions also make **ad layout** (above-tool and sticky units) and **viewability** unusually important. Industry sources claim about **+30% RPM** from viewability optimization.
- **Premium networks multiply RPM.** Once a tools site passes 50k sessions/month, moving from AdSense to Ezoic, Mediavine or Raptive plausibly raises RPM **1.5-3x**, based on MotionInvest (Ezoic about 2x AdSense) and the German forum report (+150-300%). Mediavine and Raptive have content-quality and engagement requirements that thin tool pages may not meet. That last point is my inference.

### Gaps
- **No owner-reported (first-party) AdSense page RPM** was found for any image compressor, PDF, QR, unit-converter or word-counter site. All utility-site revenue figures are third-party estimates.
- **No first-party finance-calculator-site income report** with AdSense page RPM for 2024-2026 was found. Finance RPMs come from blog and premium-network data, not calculator-specific data.
- **No quantified Q4 vs Q1 RPM swing** (percentage) from a reliable dataset.
- **No data on the country mix of utility-tool search demand** (e.g., the share of "compress image" searches from India). That would need Semrush or Ahrefs keyword-by-country pulls.
