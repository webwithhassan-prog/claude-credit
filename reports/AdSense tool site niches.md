# Build calculators where lenders, not publishers, rank

The best-paying, least-contested niches for a new AdSense calculator site in 2025-2026 are the ones where the top search results are lenders' and law firms' own sales pages rather than NerdWallet, Bankrate or Calculator.net. Ranked, they are: reverse-mortgage (HECM) and HELOC tools; small-business financing (MCA-to-APR, invoice factoring, equipment loans); DSCR and BRRRR investor tools; calculators for the 2025-2028 US tax-law changes; health-benefit cliff tools (ACA subsidy, IRMAA, SSDI back pay); and state-by-state family-law cost estimators. Home-energy savings tools, the new RAP student-loan plan, English-language UK/Australia/Canada rule variants and German rule calculators such as Aktivrente and Grunderwerbsteuer follow. Niche and geography matter more than raw traffic. A finance-heavy calculator catalog earns roughly **$21 per 1,000 visits** against about **$3** for a pure utility tool (both third-party estimates), and a US visitor is worth **5-50x** a South Asian one. AdSense approval depends on publisher content more than on how many tools a site has. Every page that shows ads needs sourced explanatory text. The site also needs a Google-certified consent tool, the privacy-policy disclosures AdSense requires, and ads kept away from calculator inputs. In Google Search, AI Overviews (organic click-through rate down **61%**) and Gemini's on-the-fly calculators are taking over single-formula queries, so rankings favor multi-input tools built on rules that differ by state or country. The owner's v1 plan fits this evidence well. **Eleven of its 20 tools target search results the researchers directly observed to be lender-held, small-site or only moderately contested**, but the Debt & Credit category is the weakest bet. Deferring the reverse-mortgage calculator also drops the strongest single opportunity for a reason unrelated to the market. Every figure here needs caution. The researchers could not open most source pages, used up their search budget and obtained **no per-keyword search volume, difficulty or CPC data**.

## Almost every figure here arrived secondhand

The research ran under unusually tight constraints. An egress proxy blocked direct page fetches for nearly every domain the researchers tried, including Google's AdSense and Search Central help pages, WordStream, LocaliQ, Semrush, Ahrefs, the IRS, Reddit and Indie Hackers. As a result, **almost every finding comes from search-result summaries, not from reading the full pages**. GitHub was the one reachable domain, so the Public Suffix List and the IAB consent-framework specification were checked directly ([Public Suffix List](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/publicsuffix/list/main/public_suffix_list.dat)). A shared budget of 200 web searches ran out partway through. That left about half of the English finance candidates unchecked, including credit utilization, debt-to-income and avalanche-vs-snowball, along with several non-English markets. Every keyword-tool domain was blocked, so **no search volume, keyword difficulty or CPC figure exists for any specific keyword in this report**.

The summaries sometimes disagree, which is a reason to distrust exact decimals. Two summaries of the same WordStream 2025 study give legal-services CPC as $9.21 and as $8.58 ([WordStream 2025](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks)). Aggregators publish "AdSense RPM" figures for Norway ($7.14) and Switzerland ($6.07) that exactly match a YouTube music dataset, so those labels are almost certainly wrong ([Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm)). The competition readings below are the report's strongest evidence, because they are direct observations even though the tool was imperfect. The payout figures are its weakest.

## Search ad prices rank the verticals, but visitor geography sets the payout

On Google Search, the most expensive advertiser industries are **Attorneys & Legal Services ($9.87 average CPC), Home & Home Improvement ($8.33) and Dentists ($8.00)**. The all-industry average is **$5.42**, according to WordStream/LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark of 13,474 US campaigns ([WordStream 2026](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2026-google-ads-benchmarks)). Finance & Insurance looks cheap in that dataset at $3.44. Aggregator lists of individual keywords, however, put the top CPCs at **$54.91 for insurance, $47.12 for mortgage and $47.07 for attorney terms** ([ppc.io](https://ppc.io/blog/high-cpc-keywords)). The researchers' explanation, which is their reading rather than a stated finding, is that the benchmark mostly samples small local advertisers, while national lenders and insurers bid on the high-intent terms that sit next to calculator queries. Education averaged $6.23 in 2025 and fell 22.79% in 2026. Healthcare search CPC averages $5.64 ([LocaliQ healthcare](https://localiq.com/blog/healthcare-search-advertising-benchmarks/)). Together these support an order of value from highest to lowest: legal, insurance, mortgage and loans, B2B software, home improvement, education, real estate, health, automotive, then travel and entertainment.

Those are prices advertisers pay, not what publishers earn. AdSense for Content pays publishers **80% of revenue after the buying platform's fee**. Google Ads keeps about 15%, so publishers net roughly **68% of advertiser spend** ([AdSense revenue share](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/180195?hl=en)). AdSense also switched to paying per impression in 2024. Display inventory sells for far less than search ads. One vendor, probably using 2022 data, reported that US publishers earned **$0.61 per click**, about a ninth of the Search average ([Partnerkin](https://partnerkin.com/en/blog/articles/adsense_rpm_rates_by_country)). A calculator page next to a $50 insurance query does not earn $34 a click. Search CPC is useful only for ranking verticals against each other.

The tier ranges are the researchers' synthesis of vendor data, not measurements. The gap between tiers A and D is roughly **7-10x per visit**. Omni Calculator grew from 20,000 to more than 18 million monthly visits on AdSense revenue and hired about 70 staff ([Google for Publishers](https://www.google.com/ads/publisher/stories/omni_calculator/)). A third-party estimate of $500k a month on 23.1M visits implies about **$21.6 per 1,000 visits** ([BoringCashCow](https://boringcashcow.com/view/calculators-website-generates-231-million-visits-a-month)). A free image resizer implies about **$2.8** ([BoringCashCow](https://boringcashcow.com/view/boring-business-free-online-image-resizer-makes-100k-a-year)). At $2-3 per 1,000 pageviews, $1,000 a month takes 350,000-500,000 pageviews; at $20 it takes about 50,000. One UK take-home-pay calculator was estimated at 150,000 visitors and $7,500 a year in its tenth month. That works out to about **$4 per 1,000 visitors** ([MyPayCalculator report](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/25154711-10-month-old-calculator-website-already-earning-7-5k-year?tab=book)), a reminder that new single-niche sites start near the bottom of every range. One vendor claims finance earns 3.0x the average site and that **new sites earn 30-40% less** until they build authority ([Adstimate](https://adstimate.com/blog/highest-paying-adsense-niches.html)). Premium ad networks such as Mediavine (50,000 sessions a month minimum) quote personal-finance RPMs of $15-40 per 1,000 sessions ([ToolSignal](https://toolsignal.site/articles/blog-display-ad-rpm-by-niche-2026)). A 2023 study found AdSense earning $6.62 per 1,000 visitors (EPMV) against Ezoic's $12.92 ([MotionInvest](https://www.motioninvest.com/finding-the-best-ad-network-changing-platforms-can-increase-website-revenue-by-300/)).

Geography moves revenue as much as niche does. Vendor tier models put the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Germany at $15-45 RPM, and India, Pakistan and the Philippines at $0.50-5 ([Adstimate by country](https://adstimate.com/blog/adsense-rpm-by-country.html)). Median US finance RPM is cited at about $25, against about $5 for Indian finance traffic ([ArbHunter](https://arbhunter.dev/blog/best-countries-ad-arbitrage-2026)). As an illustration, assume $10 RPM for US traffic and $0.50 for Indian traffic. A site with 30% US and 70% Indian traffic then blends to about $3.35, and reversing the mix gives $7.15. Wealthy non-English countries do not automatically pay US rates for pages in their own language. A German-language site owner reports **€3-7 RPM** ([Builder Society](https://www.buildersociety.com/threads/best-display-ad-companies-for-german-website-sharing-my-rpms-with-raptive.7312/)). A Berlin blog earned €2.40 while about 30% of visitors declined the cookie banner ([Global Fin Info](https://www.global-fin-info.com/advertising-platforms-for-websites-in-germany/)). Older single-site data put English above $1 per 1,000 views, German and French below $0.5 and Japanese near $0.2 ([Y. Bierling](https://www.ybierling.com/en/blog-marketing-adsense-cpm-rates-by-country)). The implication is an inference, but all the evidence points the same way: English pages read by UK, Canadian, Australian and Nordic visitors are likely the cheapest non-US revenue, ahead of translated pages.

Some high-CPC topics are traps. Google restricts online-gambling content outside 18 allowed countries (policy updated March 2025) ([AdSense policy change log](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9336650?hl=en)). It requires crypto advertisers to be certified and licensed ([MegaDigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/google-ads-crypto-content-policy/)), and payday-loan ads are prohibited in many regions. Crypto-profit, betting-odds, payday, blood-alcohol and drug-dosage calculators therefore draw fewer advertisers and carry more approval risk. Tool pages also have a viewability problem, because sessions are short and users scroll little. Sticky ad units reach more than 80% viewability, against a 67.9% global display average ([Publift](https://www.publift.com/blog/ad-viewability-optimization)). That lever has to be balanced against Google's accidental-click rules, covered below.

Semrush data adds that finance has the highest share of medium-difficulty keywords of any industry it studied, at 63% ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-competition/)). The three lender-held niches lead because they have the cleanest competition profile. For the reverse-mortgage query, the top nine results were specialist and exact-match sites such as reverse.mortgage and freehecmcalculator.com, with no NerdWallet, Bankrate or AARP ([reverse.mortgage](https://reverse.mortgage/limits); [FreeHECMCalculator](https://freehecmcalculator.com/reverse-mortgage-calculator/)). The audience is homeowners aged 62 and over, a historically valuable lead-generation group. The **2026 HECM limit of $1,249,125** and HUD's principal limit factor tables give the page a reason to be updated every year. All nine invoice-factoring results are factoring companies ([Riviera Finance](https://www.rivierafinance.com/factoring-calculator/)). The MCA results are small funders, law firms and Nav, several framed as converting a factor rate into an APR ([Nav](https://www.nav.com/business-loan-calculators/merchant-cash-advance-apr-calculator/)). DSCR results are almost entirely lenders ([Figure](https://www.figure.com/dscr-loan/dscr-calculator/)). Equipment-loan results are SoFi plus commercial and agricultural lenders ([SoFi](https://www.sofi.com/calculators/equipment-loan-calculator/)). The risk is volume. The researchers suspect the B2B and investor terms have small search demand but could not measure it.

The tax, benefits and student-loan niches (4, 5 and 8) are windows opened by rule changes, and each fills with exact-match competitors within weeks to months. The OBBBA (the 2025 federal tax law) created deductions claimed on the new Schedule 1-A: **up to $25,000 of tips**, phasing out above $150,000 MAGI ($300,000 joint); **up to $12,500 of overtime premium** ($25,000 joint); **up to $10,000 a year of car-loan interest** for 2025-2028 on new US-assembled vehicles, phasing out above $100,000/$200,000 MAGI; and an enhanced senior deduction ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-published-schedule-taxpayers-will-use-to-claim-deductions-on-no-tax-on-tips-no-tax-on-overtime-no-tax-on-car-loans-no-tax-on-seniors)). Exact-match domains already rank for them ([autoloandeductiblecalculator.com](https://autoloandeductiblecalculator.com/); [notaxonovertimecalculators.org](https://notaxonovertimecalculators.org/)), alongside TurboTax, H&R Block and Fidelity on the overtime query. The RAP student-loan plan launched on **1 July 2026**, with payments from $10 up to 10% of AGI less $50 per dependent and forgiveness after 360 payments. Its search results are all specialist or new sites, including the exact-match rapplancalc.com ([Student Loan Planner](https://www.studentloanplanner.com/repayment-assistance-plan-rap-calculator/)).

ACA subsidy tools must track legislation that is still moving. The enhanced credits expired on 31 December 2025, bringing back the 400% poverty-line cliff. A three-year extension, H.R. 1834, passed the House 230-196 on 8 January 2026 but was stalled in the Senate as of August ([acacalc](https://acacalc.com/); [CRS](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48290)). The 2026 IRMAA surcharges start at $109,000 single or $218,000 joint, on top of a $202.90 standard Part B premium ([ustax.tools](https://ustax.tools/irmaa-calculator/)). SSDI results belong to disability law firms, one of which markets its tool as "built by disability attorneys" ([Crossroads Disability](https://crossroadsdisability.com/disability-calculators/back-pay)). That is a trust signal a new site cannot easily match.

The legal and home-energy niches (6 and 7) have the weakest competition observed anywhere, but they carry more risk. A free netlify.app subdomain ranks for "divorce cost calculator" ([divorce-calculator.netlify.app](https://divorce-calculator.netlify.app/)), and Quora ranks for DUI cost. These tools are the most sensitive "Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL) content, and the formulas differ by state. Only Illinois, New York and Texas have statutory alimony formulas ([divorce.law](https://divorce.law/guides/alimony-calculator-guide/)). Child support follows one of three models, with 41 states using income shares ([StateCalc](https://statecalc.com/blog/child-support-guide/)). Energy savings depend on local prices. A heat pump saves about $344 a year in Georgia, while replacing an efficient gas furnace in a cold climate zone "might never break even without incentives" ([PickHVAC](https://www.pickhvac.com/hvac/furnace-vs-heat-pump-cost/)). EV charging costs 40-65% less than fuel, depending on the state ([Coltura](https://coltura.org/ev-vs-gas-calculator/)).

In the English-speaking markets outside the US, head terms are saturated, but recent rule changes have opened narrower variants. UK stamp-duty results mix MoneySavingExpert, estate agents and four or more exact-match domains ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/mortgages/stamp-duty/)), so the openings are edge cases such as second-home, non-resident and limited-company surcharges. Australia moved HECS to a marginal repayment system in 2025-26, with nothing payable below $67,000 ([FairWork Mate](https://fairworkmate.com.au/blog/hecs-repayment-changes-1-july-2026-marginal-system)), and dropped the 5% Deposit Scheme's income caps on 1 October 2025 ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-10-01/first-homebuyer-scheme-explainer-income-limits-property-caps/105838164)). Canada now lets insured first-time buyers take 30-year amortization ([nesto](https://www.nesto.ca/mortgage-basics/stress-test-calculator/)), though one search snippet contradicted this, so confirm it with CMHC before building.

Several head terms should be avoided outright because brands hold them. Discover, Bankrate and NerdWallet hold the top three results for balance transfer ([Discover](https://www.discover.com/credit-cards/credit-card-calculator/balance-transfer-calculator/)). Roth conversion results are brokerages only ([Schwab](https://www.schwab.com/ira/ira-calculators/roth-ira-conversion)). Life-insurance needs belong to insurers and big publishers ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/insurance/life/learn/how-much-life-insurance-do-i-need)). Car and homeowners insurance cost are brand-held too, although a Florida-only exact-match site ranks for home insurance ([flhomeinsuranceestimate.com](https://flhomeinsuranceestimate.com/)), which hints at state-level long tails. Long-distance moving cost belongs to van lines ([Allied](https://www.allied.com/moving-resources/tools-forms/long-distance-moving-calculator)). Employee-cost and break-even ROAS results are crowded with free tools that software companies use to capture leads, and coast FIRE is easy to rank for but pays little ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/investing/calculators/coast-fire-calculator)).

### Other languages reward rule changes, not head terms

Non-English markets are not the open field they are often said to be. In all ten markets checked (Germany, France, the Netherlands, Poland, Norway, Italy, Spain, Japan, Brazil and Korea), **five to nine dedicated calculator sites with "2026" in their titles already compete** for the main salary and tax calculators. They share the results with payroll software, job boards, law firms and official government calculators, such as the Austrian Finance Ministry's gross-to-net calculator ([BMF](https://bmf.gv.at/presse/pressemeldungen/2025/dezember/brutto-netto-rechner.html)). Korea is a poor fit, because Naver held **62.86%** of Korean search in 2025 against Google's 29.55% ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20260104/naver-tops-60-in-koreas-search-market-data)). The researchers rank the language markets as German, Japanese, French, Italian and then Norwegian. They deprioritize Polish and Latin American Spanish and Portuguese (large but low-RPM) and Arabic (only YouTube data).

The openings are rules that changed recently. Germany's Aktivrente, in force since 1 January 2026, lets people who keep working past retirement age earn up to **€2,000 a month tax-free** under § 3 Nr. 21 EStG, and every result for it is a small site ([smart-rechner.de](https://www.smart-rechner.de/aktivrente/rechner.php)). German property transfer tax (Grunderwerbsteuer) ranges from 3.5% in Bavaria to 6.5% in states such as NRW, so each state needs its own calculation ([finanz-tools.de](https://www.finanz-tools.de/grunderwerbsteuer-rechner)). In Japan, the tax-free income threshold (年収の壁) rises from ¥1.6M to ¥1.78M and a new childcare-support levy (子ども・子育て支援金) begins in fiscal 2026 ([sharoushi-cloud.com](https://sharoushi-cloud.com/kyuyo-keisan-tool/)), which makes take-home-pay searches by salary level (for example 年収500万 手取り) a live long tail. France raised the employer contribution on negotiated-severance payments (rupture conventionnelle) from 30% to 40% on the exempt part, which is capped at €96,120 ([boncalcul.fr](https://www.boncalcul.fr/outils/simulateur-rupture-conventionnelle)), and its notary-fee transfer duties vary by département from 5.09% to 6.30% ([mesfraisnotaire.fr](https://www.mesfraisnotaire.fr/)). Italy applies a new ISEE means-test calculation from March 2026 ([fyscal.it](https://fyscal.it/strumenti/calcolo-assegno-unico/)). Mortgage head terms, by contrast, belong to banks and brokers, as in the Netherlands ([Hypotheekshop](https://www.hypotheekshop.nl/hypotheek-berekenen/maximale-hypotheek-berekenen/)), and Japan's hometown-tax donation calculators (furusato nozei) belong to the donation portals.

On mechanics, AdSense supports about 44 languages ([AdSense languages](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9727?hl=en)). In June 2025 Google said AI translation is not automatically spam ([Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-softens-automated-translation-stance-39579.html)), but low-value translated pages still fall under the scaled-content policy. Google recommends a separate URL for each language, with hreflang tags and no redirects based on IP address ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites)). Country-specific rule tools must be built natively rather than translated. An English calculator cannot become a tool that covers 16 German states or 101 French départements.

## AdSense approval depends on page content, not tool count

AdSense's formal requirements are few. Applicants must be 18 or older, control the site's HTML head, publish mainly in a supported language and follow program policies ([AdSense eligibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en)). Rejections come from the subjective rules. Google bars ads on screens "without publisher-content or with low-value content." Its examples include a flashlight app, a pure utility where the user looks away from the screen, and it says not to place ads "on automatically generated content without manual review or curation" ([Publisher-content policy](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11112688?hl=en)). A calculator that is only input fields and a button is exactly that kind of screen. AdSense also has a "Valuable inventory: templated page" violation that fits programmatically generated tool pages ([MonetizeMore](https://www.monetizemore.com/blog/how-deal-with-valuable-inventory-templated-page-violation-adsense/)). For money and health sites, a guide by volunteer Product Experts on the AdSense community forum says reviewers apply "a special, more demanding standard." That guide is informative but is not official policy ([AdSense Community guide](https://support.google.com/adsense/community-guide/241032356/how-can-you-solve-the-low-value-content-adsense-disapproval-challenge?hl=en)).

Google sets no threshold for tool count, word count, traffic or site age, and the anecdotes are humbling. Toolpod, a developer-utilities site with **65+ tools**, explanations, FAQs, 15 blog posts, an About page and a privacy policy, was still rejected for "low value content" ([Toolpod](https://toolpod.dev/blog/adsense-rejection-low-value-content)). Another developer reported that Google seems to want 3-6 months of site history and some existing visitors ([dev.to](https://dev.to/bdubs/i-applied-for-adsense-and-got-rejected-for-low-value-content-hog)). The researchers' summary of community advice is 15-30 finished tool pages carrying several hundred to 1,000+ words of page-specific text each, 10-20 in-depth guides, trust pages, and a few months of site age with some organic traffic. Nobody guarantees approval at that level, so treat it as a floor rather than a target.

The consent and placement rules affect revenue directly. A generic cookie banner does not meet Google's requirement. Without a certified TCF consent tool, visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland cannot be shown personalized ads. Placement is where tool sites uniquely stumble. Google warns that buttons, dropdowns and navigation near ads cause accidental clicks, and that even an unintentional layout can draw a violation. That rule collides with viewability. On a short-session tool page, the slots next to the tool and its result are both the most viewable and the most likely to break policy. Sticky or anchor units and units placed below the explanation of the result are the safer compromise, as long as mobile anchor ads do not cover the input fields.

The researchers' recommended sequence is to build the tools and guides, publish the trust and legal pages, confirm in Search Console that the pages are indexed, wait two to four months or more for organic traffic, set up the consent tool and ads.txt, and only then apply. After a "low value content" rejection, do not reapply immediately. Strengthen the thinnest pages first, prune or noindex stub pages, and add original data. The Toolpod owner turned to Ezoic as a fallback. The notes also flag, without verifying, payment-threshold and US tax-withholding rules for publishers outside the US. Check AdSense's payment and tax help before the first payout.

## Search rewards maintained rule tables as AI takes over simple math

Google's March 2024 scaled-content-abuse policy targets "many pages … generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings," "no matter how it's created." Its examples include AI generation, scraping and automated translation ([Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). SEO commentary on the March 2026 enforcement wave singled out **"data-template pages that swap location names … into identical page structures"** ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/scaled-content-abuse-google-march-update-ai-pages-decimated)). That is exactly what lazy 50-state calculator sites look like. Yet programmatic state sites such as statecalc.com, supportbystate.com and workcompcalc.com still ranked in September 2026 ([StateCalc](https://statecalc.com/alimony/); [WorkCompCalc](https://workcompcalc.com/workers-comp-settlement-calculator/)). The dividing line is whether the pages actually differ. A state page that runs that state's schedule, cites the statute and shows a worked example adds value. A page that only inserts the state name into a shared formula is a target. The helpful-content system now runs inside Google's core ranking system. Core updates rolled out in March, June and December 2025 and in March and May 2026 ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-may-2026-core-update-rollout-is-now-complete-479119)).

Established calculator sites have weathered these updates better than blogs. Omni took a slight hit in the March 2023 core update and recovered in April ([Sistrix](https://www.sistrix.com/blog/google-core-update-march-2023/)). Of the 130 sites hit hardest by the September 2023 helpful content update, 129 kept declining ([Lily Ray](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_seo-helpfulcontentupdate-hcu-activity-7162899994818068481-SypY)). Calculator.net draws about **56M monthly visits** ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/the-free-tools-seo-strategy/)), and one estimator puts roughly **38% of it as direct traffic** ([SiteWorthTraffic](https://www.siteworthtraffic.com/report/calculator.net)). That is a brand-and-bookmark moat no newcomer has.

AI answers are now the bigger threat. On queries that show an AI Overview, organic CTR fell **61%**, from 1.76% to 0.61% ([Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update)). Ahrefs measured a **58%** drop for the first-ranked result in December 2025 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update)), Pew found users clicked a result on 8% of visits that showed an AI summary against 15% without one ([Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)), and **68.01%** of Google searches ended without a click in early 2026 ([SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/)). AI Overviews are also spreading past informational queries, whose share of triggers fell from 91.3% to 57.1% during 2025 as commercial and transactional queries took up the rest ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews-commercial-search-study/)). Most directly relevant, Gemini 3's launch on 18 November 2025 showed Google building a custom interactive loan calculator inside AI Mode to compare two mortgage options ([Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-3-search-ai-mode/)), a capability since expanded into AI Overviews ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-expands-generative-ui-beyond-ai-mode-into-ai-overviews/586452/)). In early September 2026, AI Overviews began loading an interactive character counter for "character counter" queries ([Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-character-counter-42026.html)).

There is a counter-signal. On queries without an AI Overview, organic CTR rose from 2.93% to 3.97% during 2025. Seer attributes this to those queries skewing toward people with no single universal answer, who still click through ([Seer 2026](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update)).

Four kinds of tools hold up against this: those built on rule tables that change every year (IRMAA brackets, the ACA poverty-line cliff, HECM limits, state child-support schedules, OBBBA phase-outs); those built on proprietary or local price data, like MoneyGeek's home-insurance estimator drawn from 38,115 quotes ([MoneyGeek](https://www.moneygeek.com/insurance/homeowners/calculator-estimate-home-insurance-cost/)); planners that compare multiple scenarios; and tools whose output people take to a lender or lawyer. What a disposable calculator generated inside an AI answer lacks is exactly what makes these pages durable: rich outputs such as amortization tables, charts, CSV and PDF export and shareable result URLs; trust signals such as dated sources, FAQs under the tool (which Ahrefs recommends) and named calculator authors (which Omni publishes); and an embed code that earns links. Google's long-standing guidance is to keep widget links branded and nofollowed ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2016/09/a-reminder-about-widget-links)), and Redfin's embeddable calculators reportedly earned one page 5.7K backlinks from 2.1K domains ([Link Building Journal](https://linkbuildingjournal.co.uk/interactive-calculators-100-links/)). Brands cited inside AI Overviews earned 35% more organic clicks ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-drive-drop-organic-paid-ctr-464212)), which favors pages with clear, citable methodology.

On site structure, the Google document leak of 2024 showed that Google stores site-focus attributes (siteFocusScore, siteRadius), although how much they weigh in ranking is unknown ([Hobo Web](https://www.hobo-web.co.uk/topical-authority/)). Omni took about a decade to reach 3,700 calculators. Both point a new domain toward a focused hub of related tools rather than a broad calculator catalog. AdSense-only income is fragile. One Indie Hackers creator saw monthly AdSense revenue fall from $800 to $200 at the same traffic ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/from-200-to-3k-month-why-i-ditched-adsense-for-direct-ad-sales-with-numbers-d1098e3aae)). Photopea, the best-documented ad-funded free tool, reached about $3M a year only after years of zero revenue ([BuiltPlain](https://www.builtplain.com/photopea-free-app-ad-revenue/)). Plan to move to a premium ad network at around 50,000 sessions a month, and keep direct sponsorships in reserve.

## V1's weak spots are debt tools and the missing HECM calculator

The v1 plan matches the evidence on its three biggest choices. It picks one high-value vertical instead of a broad calculator catalog, it targets US English traffic first, and it favors multi-input finance tools built on rule tables that must be maintained. Going tool by tool, **11 of the 20 target search results the researchers directly observed** to be lender-held, small-site or only moderately contested. Debt consolidation sits in a medium field, balance transfer sits in results locked up by Discover, Bankrate and NerdWallet, and **seven tools were never checked**: credit card payoff, avalanche vs snowball, debt-to-income, credit utilization, home equity borrowing limit, no tax on tips and the senior deduction.

| Debt & Credit (6 tools) | Card issuers bid heavily. Debt-consolidation results are credit unions, mortgagecalculator.org and calculator.net ([Calculator.net](https://www.calculator.net/debt-consolidation-calculator.html)) | Balance transfer is locked. Four tools are unchecked. Debt-to-income and credit utilization are one-line ratios, the kind of query Google answers directly | Weakest category. Keep it as a supporting cluster and point it at home-equity decisions |

Debt & Credit has the thinnest evidence and the most exposure to AI answers. The researchers list "what is DTI" among queries Google answers directly, and credit utilization is a single division. These tools are cheap to build and help with internal linking and the page count for approval, but they should not be counted on for traffic. They will do more if they lead into the home-equity cluster. For example, frame debt-to-income around HELOC or cash-out qualification, and turn balance transfer into a comparison with HELOC-based consolidation. Citizens Bank's "HELOC debt consolidation" calculator shows this angle is a live search ([Citizens](https://www.citizensbank.com/home-equity-loans/heloc-debt-consolidation-calculator.aspx)). Before investing more in the six Debt & Credit tools, run them through Keyword Planner.

The largest cost in the plan is deferring the reverse-mortgage calculator. It was the researchers' top pick in the consumer mortgage space, and it was deferred because HUD's principal limit factor tables could not be downloaded in this environment. That is a tooling limitation, not evidence about the market. HUD publishes the tables, and they can be obtained outside the sandbox. The HECM suite should ship as v1.1 rather than wait for a v2. The cash-out-vs-HELOC tool needs particular depth, such as closing costs, rate scenarios, a break-even point, an amortization table and shareable results, because Google's own showcase for generated calculators is a comparison of two mortgage options.

The tax category has a timing problem. The deductions apply to tax years 2025-2028, which by this report's arithmetic leaves about three filing seasons (2027, 2028 and 2029). Searches peak from January to April, which is when ad RPM is seasonally weakest after the fourth-quarter peak ([Mediavine](https://www.mediavine.com/blog/ad-revenue-seasons/); [Refinery89](https://refinery89.com/q1-rpm-drop-what-publishers-can-do-about-it/)). If the site launches in late 2026 and waits the anecdotal 3-6 months before applying, the 2027 peak will arrive before or during AdSense review. The first season's value is therefore traffic, links and approval signals, not ad revenue. The four single-deduction tools compete with single-purpose exact-match sites. A combined Schedule 1-A tool that stacks tips, overtime, car interest and the senior deduction, with MAGI phase-outs, is the differentiator the researchers proposed. Check the $6,000 senior deduction amount and its phase-out against IRS guidance before shipping, because the notes did not confirm them.

The whole site falls under YMYL, so the trust layer is not optional. Twenty tools fall inside the anecdotal 15-30 range, but Toolpod's rejection with 65+ tools shows that count alone does not get approval. Add 10-20 guides that explain topics such as the 2026 ACA cliff, factor rate vs APR and HELOC vs cash-out. Add named reviewers with relevant credentials, a methodology page and primary-source citations. The business tools have the cleanest competition profile but unknown volume. Their fit inside a "personal & small-business finance" hub is sound, so the open question is scale, not whether they belong.

Revenue expectations should be modest. At the researchers' $20 illustrative RPM for tier-A finance, $1,000 a month needs about 50,000 US pageviews. After the vendor-claimed 30-40% new-site discount, the same traffic yields about $600-700. The UK pay-calculator example suggests early per-visitor earnings can sit far lower.

Deferring non-English markets is well supported: local-language RPM is anecdotally €3-7 in Germany, about 30% of visitors may withhold consent, and head terms are crowded in every market checked. Two refinements are worth considering. English variants for the UK, Australia and Canada are a cheaper intermediate step, since they need no translation and probably pay more than German-language pages. Japanese also deserves a look as the challenger to German-first. Japan has no GDPR consent loss, frequent tax-rule changes and an anecdotal $8 finance-blog RPM ([Dicloak](https://dicloak.com/blog-detail/google-adsense-rpm-by-country-2025-how-to-maximize-revenue-across-countries)), though it needs native-quality Japanese and the tax-accountant review (税理士監修) that ranking Japanese pages advertise.

Every item needs a Keyword Planner volume check before it is built, because volume is the gap no research here could close.

The ordering reflects a few judgments. Items 2 and 3 come before larger new areas because they reuse code already built for v1 and face the same weak competition. Divorce-cost and alimony tools answer money questions, so they sit closer to a finance site's focus than DUI or workers' comp. That keeps the legal pair at the bottom despite their weak competition. Car-accident settlement calculators are left off entirely. The results are law-firm pages, and the topic is the most sensitive YMYL content in the research. The standing "do not build" list is: the head terms for balance transfer, Roth conversion, life, car and homeowners insurance, and moving cost; crypto, betting, payday and blood-alcohol tools; and pure utilities, which Google now renders inside its own answers.

## Conclusion

The most durable result of this research is a method rather than a list. When lenders, law firms or small exact-match sites hold the top results instead of big publishers, advertiser demand is high and editorial competition is weak. Paired with a Keyword Planner volume check, that rule works as a repeatable filter for every future tool. The filter is needed because specific winners do not last. Every 2025-26 rule change the researchers examined, from overtime and car-loan deductions to RAP and Aktivrente, attracted exact-match competitors within weeks to months. The site's advantage has to come from speed, depth and upkeep, not from being first.

The same asset protects against both of the site's main threats. A maintained, sourced calendar of rule updates covers the annual HECM limit, IRMAA brackets, the poverty-line tables, the OBBBA phase-outs and, later, the rates for each German state. Google's on-the-fly calculators cannot match it, and it gives YMYL-skeptical reviewers the evidence they look for. The site should see itself as a small publisher of regulatory data that presents it as calculators, and should budget the annual refresh as its core cost. Timing also matters. Tax-tool traffic peaks in the first quarter, when RPM is lowest and approval may still be pending. The first filing season will therefore build authority rather than revenue. The fourth quarter of 2027, when RPM peaks, is the realistic first window in which the full catalog, including HECM, can be live, approved and earning.
